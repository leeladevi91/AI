Document Match? Why

5 Incremental RAG = your Data Engineering skills
When a policy changes from 30 days to 15 days, update the affected document text and vectors. Do not rebuild every
document unnecessarily.
Data Engineering AI / RAG equivalent
CDC / change events Detect new, updated, deleted documents
Primary key Stable document ID and chunk ID
MERGE / UPSERT Update stored vectors and associated text
Watermark / version Ignore stale, out-of-order updates
Deduplication / retries Avoid duplicate chunks and repeated processing
Data quality / reconciliation Validate source text; detect missing or stale vectors
6 30-second interview explanation
“An LLM generates answers. In RAG, we first retrieve relevant information from external sources, such as company
documents, and provide it as context to the LLM. We often split documents into chunks, create embeddings, and use vector
search to find relevant passages. The data pipeline must keep documents, embeddings, and metadata current as sources
change.”
7 Quick self-check
1. Who generates the final answer: the vector database or the LLM?
2. What turns text into numerical vectors?
3. Does RAG always require a vector database?
4. What should happen when a policy document is updated?
Answers: 1) LLM. 2) Embedding model. 3) No—keyword or hybrid retrieval also works. 4) Update the changed text,
embeddings, and index; remove obsolete chunks.
